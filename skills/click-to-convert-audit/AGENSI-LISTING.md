**Display name**
Click-to-Convert: Landing Page Audit for Paid Ads

**Tagline (one line)**
Will this landing page convert your paid traffic? Get a verdict and the exact fixes — handed straight to your coding agent.

**Short description**
Audits a landing page for how well it will convert Google Ads traffic, then outputs two things: a short OK/FIX scorecard for you, and a Cursor-ready change brief for your coding agent. Checks the post-click stuff generic CRO tools miss — message match against your actual ad and keywords, landing-page-experience signals, and the hard stops (tracking, broken form, speed) that quietly drain ad spend.

**What it does**
- Runs a caps-first audit: tracking integrity, form function, mobile speed, and security gate the verdict before anything else is scored.
- Tier 1 (URL only): conversion read — five-second clarity, trust & proof, friction, copy.
- Tier 2 (URL + your ad context): adds scent — does the page match the promise of your ad and the intent behind your keywords. This is the part nothing else checks.
- Runs the free PageSpeed test itself (no account needed). Uses an optional Google Ads connector only to pull ad copy + keywords for you; otherwise accepts a screenshot, paste, or template.
- Outputs a human scorecard AND a Cursor / Claude Code change brief, with a hard "do not touch" boundary so the agent never fabricates analytics wiring.

**Who it's for**
Founders, marketers, and freelancers running Google Ads (or other paid search) to a landing page who want to know if the page is ready for spend — and want the fixes in a form a coding agent can execute.

**When it triggers / example prompts**
- "Will this landing page convert? https://example.com/lp"
- "Audit this page for Google Ads: <url> — here are my headlines and keywords"
- "Is this page ready to run paid traffic to?"
- "Why isn't my landing page converting? Am I wasting ad spend?"

**Suggested category / tags**
Marketing · CRO · Google Ads / PPC · Landing pages · Conversion

**Compatibility note**
Works in Cursor, Claude Code, and Claude.ai. Mobile speed uses the free public
PageSpeed API. A Google Ads data connector is optional (it just auto-fills your
ad copy and keywords for the Tier 2 scent check); without it, paste a screenshot
or fill the short template.
